## Quest briefing

An ML-driven router management system built at Technata Hacks 2024, where
it took **3rd place**.

- Built the control system in Flask, reducing power consumption by ~20%
  under simulated network workloads.
- Trained and validated models on live telemetry data with Scikit-learn,
  improving stability while cutting excess heat and energy waste.
- Paired with a Next.js dashboard showing real-time router activity,
  energy savings, and heat insights for IT administrators.

## Loot (what was learned)

- Feature engineering on telemetry data matters more than model choice
  for problems like this.
- The gap between "model works in a notebook" and "model drives a control
  loop" is where the real engineering lives.
- First hackathon podium — proof that a tight demo story wins judges.
