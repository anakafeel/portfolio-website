## Quest briefing

A self-hosted homelab designed security-first for sensitive data, running
on a Raspberry Pi in the corner of the room.

- Services are exposed **only** via Cloudflare Tunnels — zero inbound
  ports open on the network edge.
- Containerized every service with Docker for isolation, reproducibility,
  and controlled access.
- Nginx reverse proxying plus local DNS handle secure internal routing,
  service discovery, and name resolution.

## Loot (what was learned)

- Zero-trust networking in practice: outbound-only tunnels remove an
  entire class of attack surface.
- ARM quirks: not every container image loves a Raspberry Pi.
- DNS is always the problem. Even at home. *Especially* at home.
